# Taria

**A private, federated knowledge system for studying the world as a collection of structured, connected domains.**

> **Visibility:** Taria's canonical source repository is private and not publicly accessible. This page is a public overview of the project, not its source code, dataset, or internal documentation.

Taria is my long-running attempt to build a durable intellectual infrastructure: a place where ideas, evidence, concepts, institutions, organisms, technologies, languages, and creative systems can be studied in depth **without becoming disconnected collections of notes**.

It treats research as something that can be structured, questioned, revised, connected, and eventually made computational.

## Why it exists

Traditional notes are easy to accumulate and difficult to build upon. Over time, the same concepts get described repeatedly, provenance disappears, distinctions blur, and individual research projects become isolated silos.

Taria takes a different approach. Instead of organizing knowledge primarily by document or folder, it organizes it by **ontological responsibility**: which domain owns a concept, what kind of thing it is, what evidence supports it, and how it relates to things studied elsewhere.

The aspiration is not a single all-knowing database. It's an extensible **research federation** where different disciplines can maintain their own internal structure while participating in a larger intellectual system.

## How it's organized

The basic unit is a **tarium**: a bounded intellectual domain with its own ontology, research procedures, specimens, and evolving body of work.

Individual taria can develop independently. A thin federation layer records their identities, boundaries, and explicit relationships, without forcing every field into one universal schema.

```text
                    TARIA
             research federation
                       |
         +-------------+-------------+
         |             |             |
      Tarium        Tarium        Tarium
     ontology      ontology      ontology
     evidence      evidence      evidence
     research      research      research
         \             |             /
          +------------+------------+
                       |
         cross-domain relationships
                       |
          derived works & publications
```

A new area can begin as a small incubating idea, mature into a specialized research domain, and connect to the rest of the federation when those connections are justified.

A few principles shape the project:

- **Conceptual independence.** Disciplines retain their own models; integration does not require flattening their differences.
- **Evidence and provenance.** Sources, factual claims, interpretations, and speculation should remain distinguishable.
- **Research as a process.** Queues, reference specimens, and evolving tasks make investigation repeatable and progressively deeper.
- **Cross-disciplinary reasoning.** Links between domains are explicit, inspectable, and open to revision.
- **Private foundations, deliberate publication.** The research substrate can stay private while selected works, software, essays, and other outputs become public.

## What it studies

Taria contains an expanding constellation of domains, including:

| Domain | Scope |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Language & linguistics** | Language systems, grammar, morphology, historical change, dialects, and linguistic representation. |
| **Law & institutions** | Jurisdictions, legislation, cases, legal reasoning, and institutional structures. |
| **Economics & finance** | Corporations, industries, production, markets, financial institutions, and economic organization. |
| **Artificial intelligence & software** | Models, methods, architectures, software systems, computational mechanisms, and their histories. |
| **Biology & engineering** | Living organisms, evolution, physical systems, mechanisms, components, and technical reconstruction. |
| **Philosophy, concepts & arguments** | Conceptual distinctions, theories, claims, inference, objections, and competing explanations. |
| **Politics & history** | Political institutions, theories, historical cases, and the structures of social power. |
| **Music, narrative & scenes** | Musical form, dramatic systems, storytelling, and structured audiovisual worlds. |

These are not merely labels for note folders. Each domain is intended to become a **living research program** with its own vocabulary, organizing principles, reference cases, and mechanisms for growth.

## From research to working systems

Taria is also part of a larger family of software and research projects. Its role is to preserve conceptual structure and domain knowledge; adjacent tools can acquire source material, process observations, produce new representations, or turn structured ideas into interactive applications.

The direction of travel looks something like this:

**Source material → structured domain knowledge → cross-domain synthesis → software, research outputs, and creative work.**

That makes Taria both an intellectual endeavor and an engineering challenge. It brings together ontology design, archival thinking, schema evolution, provenance, reproducible workflows, human judgment, and AI-assisted research.

## Status & access

**Active, evolving, and private.** Taria is a personal research infrastructure rather than a publicly available product. Its internal models and domain coverage continue to change as new research areas are instantiated and older work is incorporated.

The repository itself is **not publicly accessible**. This page exists to describe its goals and scope without publishing the private corpus or internal working materials.
